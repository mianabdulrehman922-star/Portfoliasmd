The Desi Tradition

A Tradition of Rich Desi Hospitality

At Pind Restaurant Johar Town, we celebrate the true spirit of traditional Pakistani cooking. Our kitchen brings alive authentic recipes passed down through generations—slow-cooked mutton karahis, creamy handis, flame-kissed tikkas, and freshly baked tandoori naans.

Designed for family gatherings, friendly feasts, and celebratory dinners, Pind Restaurant offers a warm, rustic, and welcoming environment right in the heart of Lahore’s vibrant Civic Centre area.
